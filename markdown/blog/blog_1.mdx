### What to watch for

Fake delivery texts usually create pressure with a missed package, unpaid toll, or account hold. The link may look official at a glance, but the safest move is to avoid tapping it.

- Do not open the link from the text.
- Go to the official company website yourself.
- Ask a trusted person if the message feels urgent.

The app is built to slow this moment down before anyone gives away payment details.
