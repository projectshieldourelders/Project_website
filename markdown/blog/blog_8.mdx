### Keep money out of new online relationships

Romance scammers build trust before creating a crisis. They may ask for travel money, medical help, gift cards, crypto, or bank transfers.

- Do not send money to someone you have not met in person.
- Talk to a trusted person before paying.
- Be careful if they ask you to keep the relationship secret.
