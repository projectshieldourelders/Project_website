### Do not hand over the screen

If an unexpected caller asks for remote access, stop. They may be trying to see passwords, move money, or install software.

- Do not install remote access tools for a caller.
- Call the official company number yourself.
- Ask a trusted person to sit with you.

Shield Our Elders treats remote access pressure as a major warning sign.
