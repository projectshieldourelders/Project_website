### Stop before buying

No bank, government office, tech support agent, or utility company should demand gift cards. If someone tells you to buy cards and read the numbers, treat it as a scam.

- Do not buy the cards.
- Do not send photos of the card numbers.
- Ask a trusted contact before paying.

The payment checker in the app is designed for exactly this moment.
