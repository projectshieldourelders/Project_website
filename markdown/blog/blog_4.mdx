### Make a phrase before you need it

AI voice scams can sound personal and emotional. A simple family verification phrase gives everyone a way to check identity without arguing.

- Pick a phrase only the family knows.
- Use it before sending money.
- Do not let the caller rush or shame you.

The app reminds users to verify family emergency claims before acting.
