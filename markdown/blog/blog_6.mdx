### The second scam can hurt too

After someone loses money, another scammer may offer recovery help. They may ask for an upfront fee, account access, or more personal information.

- Call your bank first.
- Report the scam through official channels.
- Do not pay strangers who promise recovery.

The recovery guide warns users about this pattern.
